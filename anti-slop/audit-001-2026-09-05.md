## Executive Summary
All 4 user requirements and requested fixes have been implemented and verified with automated test suites:
1. **Right Price Scale**: Upgraded from arbitrary 7 fractional divisions to full TradingView-style rounded price tick markings (`$1.00`, `$2.00`, `$5.00` round step intervals, tick notches, and subtle `#141A26` grid lines).
2. **Planned Order Preview ("Ancang-Ancang")**: Visual setup lines (dashed cyan Entry, dashed rose SL, dashed emerald TP, shaded reward/risk zones, live target profit/loss, and axis badges) rendered live on the chart prior to executing a position.
3. **Multi-Timeframe Replay Progress & Synchronization**: Switching from M1 to higher timeframes (M5, M15, etc.) preserves the current `replayTime`, session ID, active/closed trades, and balance without resetting to bar 0 or skipping candles.
4. **Dashboard & Session Integration**: Added `POST /api/backtest/sessions/:id/sync-to-journal` and direct "Buka di Dashboard" buttons in both toolbar and bottom drawer; seamlessly syncs manual sessions and closed trades to the main journal and loads `/dashboard?sessionId=...`.
